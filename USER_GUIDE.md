# User Guide — For Caregivers

## Opening the form

Tap the link your coordinator shared with you (or the icon on your home
screen if you've added it — see below). The form opens in your browser.

## Filling out a visit report

- The form is split into sections (A–S). Tap a section title to expand or
  collapse it.
- A green checkmark appears next to a section once its required fields are
  filled in.
- The bar at the top shows your overall progress.
- Fields marked with a red **\*** are required before you can submit.

## Capturing GPS location

In Section A, tap **📍 Capture GPS**. Your phone will ask for location
permission the first time — allow it. Your coordinates will fill in
automatically.

## Adding medications or items distributed

Tap **+ Add Medication** or **+ Add Item** to add another row. Tap the
**✕** next to a row to remove it.

## Signatures

In Section R, sign with your finger (or a stylus) directly on the box.
Tap **Clear** to redo a signature.

## Taking photos

In Sections B and S, tap the photo field to open your camera or choose an
existing photo. Photos are automatically resized so they upload quickly
even on a slow connection.

## If you lose signal

You can keep filling out the form — your answers are saved automatically
on your phone as you go (look for the **Offline** label at the top). When
you're back online, open the form again (your draft will still be there)
and tap **Submit Visit Report**.

> Note: submitting still requires an internet connection. The form saves
> your answers locally so you don't lose them, but it can't send them to
> the office until you're back online.

## Submitting

1. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Submit Visit Report**.
2. Confirm when asked — once submitted, you can't edit that report.
3. You'll see a confirmation message. The form will then reset for your
   next visit.

## Common issues

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This report was already submitted" | You already sent this one — no need to resend. Start a new report for the next visit. |
| GPS won't capture | Make sure location permission is allowed for your browser in your phone's Settings, and that you have a clear view of the sky if indoors GPS is weak. |
| Submit button seems stuck | Check your connection — the form needs internet to send data, even though it can save drafts offline. |
| Camera won't open | Make sure camera permission is allowed for your browser in your phone's Settings. |

## Adding the form to your home screen

**Android (Chrome)**: open the link → tap the **⋮** menu → **Add to Home
screen**.

**iPhone (Safari)**: open the link → tap the **Share** icon → **Add to
Home Screen**.

This gives you a one-tap icon like any other app.
